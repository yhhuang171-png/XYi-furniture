06 / Buyer questions

Before we build.

Practical questions for furniture importers, brands, product developers and project buyers.

Can you manufacture custom metal furniture frames?

Custom frame fabrication is part of the preliminary factory capability information. Send a drawing or reference, dimensions, materials and expected quantity so the team can confirm project feasibility and scope.

Do you support welded and knock-down furniture structures?

Welded structures appear in our existing manufacturing information, and KD structures are included in the preliminary factory brief. Joint design, assembly method and the exact production scope need technical and sample confirmation.

Can metal finishes be customized?

Share a finish reference or physical sample. The team needs to confirm the substrate, available process, color match and acceptance criteria. Catalogue colors alone do not establish a finishing method.

What information is needed for an OEM quotation?

Please provide reference images or drawings, dimensions, target materials, quantity, destination market and packaging or testing requirements. Include any specific KD or upholstery requirements.

Are the featured products verified manufacturing cases?

Not yet. The featured catalogue images show design references. Production status, material, manufacturing route, factory processing responsibility and permission to publish must be confirmed before they can be presented as verified factory cases.

What are your MOQ and sample or production lead times?

These are confirmed for the selected design, material, order quantity and production scope during quotation. Send your requested schedule for review; it is not a confirmed delivery commitment.
